## 🔥 Quick Marketing Tips

1. **Give value first** - Share 10 free prompts before selling
2. **Use social proof** - Add real testimonials ASAP
3. **Test pricing** - Try $2.99, $4.99, and $7.99
4. **Post daily** - Consistency beats perfection
5. **Respond fast** - Answer questions within 1 hour
6. **Track everything** - Know what works
7. **Start small** - $10/day ads, then scale winners

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Setting price too high ($19.99+) → Start low
❌ Not marketing enough → Need traffic!
❌ Perfectionism → Launch fast, improve later
❌ Ignoring customer feedback → Listen and adapt
❌ Not tracking metrics → Can't improve what you don't measure
❌ Giving up too early → First sale takes time
